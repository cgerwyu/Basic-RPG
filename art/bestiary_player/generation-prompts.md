# Набор промптов для иллюстраций

Все шесть иллюстраций созданы встроенным режимом `imagegen`. Изображение Тарр-Горака основано на визуальной форме из файла `Tarr-Gorak the Firstfang.bbmodel`; остальные пять сцен созданы как новые референсы по лору книги.

## Тарр-Горак Первоклык

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-width chapter illustration for a Russian fantasy bestiary PDF
Primary request: Create a polished cinematic illustration of Tarr-Gorak the Firstfang in the Stone Fang Jungle, faithfully based on the Blockbench screenshot reference.
Input images: Image 1 is the authoritative character-shape reference. Preserve the huge green horned reptilian predator silhouette, squared heavy skull, red eyes, white bone-like jaw plates and teeth, short forearms, powerful legs, and long counterbalancing tail.
Scene/backdrop: humid ancient jungle under giant roots, fang-shaped standing stones, thin amber mist, distant kharr hunting camp silhouettes.
Subject: Tarr-Gorak stalking forward from deep foliage, three-quarter side view, entire creature visible and readable.
Style/medium: premium dark-fantasy game concept art with tactile painterly realism; strong Minecraft-inspired blocky anatomy retained but rendered as high-end bestiary art.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, creature dominant, leave calmer dark foliage at upper left for later page typography, cinematic depth.
Lighting/mood: green filtered canopy light, warm amber eye-level haze, ominous but suitable for the first and easiest boss rather than apocalyptic.
Color palette: moss green, deep jade, bone ivory, muted ochre, small red eye accents.
Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no selection outlines, no anatomy redesign, no extra horns, no wings, no armor, no rider.
```
