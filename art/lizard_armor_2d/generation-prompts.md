# Lizard armor 2D concept prompts

Mode: built-in image generation with supplied item textures as references.

## Stone Fang

Use case: game texture concept sheet.

Create a clean 2D pixel-art design sheet for an ordinary Minecraft heavy armor set called Stone Fang, faithfully derived from the four supplied inventory sprites.

Show one complete armor set as flat orthographic front and back views on a transparent background. This is not a 3D model and not a rendered Minecraft screenshot: it is crisp low-resolution pixel art intended to guide a vanilla humanoid armor-layer texture.

Armor identity:

- dark emerald and forest-green lizard-scale heavy armor;
- clearly readable as forged armor, not grass camouflage: separated plates, near-black seams, hard jade highlights and restrained pale-green edge trim;
- helmet has a large open face aperture so the player’s face remains visible, with a crown/brow shaped like reptile scales;
- shoulders are armored but do not use bulky protruding cubes;
- leggings have layered thigh and shin plates;
- each boot visibly ends in exactly three ivory claws;
- no gems, no purple aura, no magical glow on the ordinary version.

Composition: centered front view and centered back view, consistent proportions, all four armor pieces visible. Pixel art only, square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no text, no labels, no scenery, no shadow, transparent background. Preserve the supplied sprites’ green palette and silhouette motifs while making the plate structure much clearer.

## Firstfang

Use case: game texture concept sheet.

Create a clean 2D pixel-art design sheet for the boss-upgraded Minecraft heavy armor set called Firstfang, faithfully based on the supplied animated item sprites and gem sheet.

Show one complete armor set as flat orthographic front and back views on a transparent background. This is not a 3D model and not a rendered Minecraft screenshot: it is crisp low-resolution pixel art intended to guide a vanilla humanoid armor-layer texture.

Base armor:

- same dark emerald/forest-green lizard-scale forged heavy armor family;
- readable separated plates, near-black seams, jade highlights, pale green edge trim;
- open face aperture;
- exactly three ivory claws on each boot;
- no bulky cubic protrusions.

Exact boss-gem placement is mandatory:

- ONE large whole green boss gem embedded only in the center of the chestplate;
- ONE small shard centered on the forehead;
- ONE small shard centered on the front belt;
- ONE small shard on the front of each boot;
- no gems on shoulders, arms, knees, thighs, back, or anywhere else.

Poison magic:

- the whole chest gem and the four shard placements have a restrained purple poisonous aura around their green cores;
- show the purple aura as a crisp pixel halo suitable for an 8-frame pulse animation;
- armor itself stays green and metallic, not purple.

Composition: centered front and back views, consistent proportions, all four pieces visible. Pixel art only, square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no text, no labels, no scenery, no shadow, transparent background. Back view has no gem.

## Stone Fang — HD material pass

Use case: stylized-concept.

Asset type: high-resolution pixel-art material and armor reference for a Minecraft humanoid armor-layer texture.

Primary request: Redesign the ordinary Stone Fang lizard heavy armor at four times the texture detail of vanilla Minecraft while preserving the established silhouette, green palette, open face and exactly three claws per boot.

Input images: Image 1 is the approved armor design reference; Image 2 is the current low-resolution UV/material reference; Images 3-5 are the original item sprites and must guide identity.

Subject: one complete ordinary heavy armor set, orthographic front and back, with isolated close-up swatches for helmet, chest plate, scale mail, bracer, greave and ivory claws.

Style/medium: crisp high-resolution pixel art, 32-bit RPG inventory/character texture quality, deliberately hand-pixelled; square pixels and hard edges.

Materials/textures: individually readable overlapping reptile scales; forged dark emerald plates; black recessed seams; thin jade edge highlights; chips and hammer marks; small bronze-green rivets; layered chest chevrons; subtle bone scratches on claws. Composition/framing: flat orthographic design sheet on transparent background; front and back views; detail swatches arranged beside them.

Constraints: no gems, no aura, no glow, no bulky 3D cubes; helmet face opening remains large; exactly three claws per boot; detail must remain readable when reduced to a Minecraft armor texture.

Avoid: smooth painting, gradients, photorealism, text, labels, scenery, antialiasing, random camouflage noise.

## Firstfang — HD material and animation pass

Use case: stylized-concept.

Asset type: high-resolution pixel-art armor and animation reference for a Minecraft boss armor-layer texture.

Primary request: Redesign the elevated Firstfang boss set at four times vanilla Minecraft texture detail. It must visibly exceed the ordinary Stone Fang set in material richness while remaining the same armor family.

Input images: Image 1 is the approved Firstfang placement concept; Image 2 is the current low-resolution UV/material reference; Images 3-5 are the original animated item and gem references.

Subject: one complete elevated heavy armor set shown orthographically front and back, plus a clear eight-frame strip demonstrating the poison gem animation.

Style/medium: crisp high-resolution hand-pixelled 32-bit RPG art; square pixels, hard edges, no antialiasing.

Armor details: layered dark emerald dragon-lizard scales, engraved jade rims, black recessed plate seams, forged scratches, tiny gold-green rivets, secondary scale rows, brighter boss-quality edge highlights.

Exact gem placement: one whole green boss gem only in the center of the chest; one shard centered on the forehead; one shard centered on the belt; one shard on the front of each boot. No gems anywhere else and no gem on the back.

Eight-frame animation: frame 1 dormant violet outline; frames 2-3 violet light spreads; frames 4-5 aura expands into jagged poison wisps and small motes; frame 6 brightest green core with vivid purple corona; frames 7-8 wisps retract and dim. The silhouette and armor stay still; only gem highlights, aura ring, poison wisps and particles move.

Color palette: deep forest green, emerald, jade, near-black, acid green gem cores, violet and magenta poison aura, ivory claws.

Constraints: open face; exactly three claws per boot; no bulky 3D geometry; animation must be obvious even on the in-game character; retain transparent background.

Avoid: smooth gradients, photorealism, text, labels, scenery, random gems, shoulder/knee gems, purple recoloring of the armor itself.
